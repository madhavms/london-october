# Covent Garden by Lamplight

An afternoon London plan for Saturday 17 October 2026, starting and ending at Horley.

Thameslink to London Bridge (meet there), Borough Market, the Thames path to Waterloo Bridge and Somerset House, Covent Garden's Piazza and market hall, Neal's Yard and Seven Dials, an early 5:45 pm dinner at Dishoom Covent Garden, Chinatown, Trafalgar Square and the Golden Jubilee footbridge at night, then the 8:14 pm Thameslink from Blackfriars, back in Horley by 9 pm.

Live: https://madhavms.github.io/london-october/

A single `index.html` with photos in `img/` (Wikimedia Commons, credited in the footer). Deployed to GitHub Pages by `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` on every push to `main`.
