3:35 pm

Borough Market, tasters only

Stalls shut 5:00 pm

London’s oldest food market, under Victorian iron and the railway arches, at its liveliest on a Saturday. Keep it light, because dinner is a big one. Share one or two things, then something sweet.

Pick two
  • Raclette at Kappacasein, melted cheese scraped onto potatoes. Share one portion.
  • Bread Ahead doughnut, custard or salted caramel, cut in half.
  • Monmouth Coffee on Stoney Street, a flat white to carry along the river.
  • Oma, tonight’s dinner, is on Bedale Street at the market’s edge, so you’ll pass the door.

4:15 pm

The Thames path to Tate Modern

Free · 20 minute walk

From the market, slip past Southwark Cathedral to the river and head west. Along Clink Street you pass the rose window of a ruined medieval bishop’s palace. Then comes a full-size replica of the Golden Hinde, Drake’s Tudor galleon, wedged into a tiny dock. Under Southwark Bridge you reach Shakespeare’s Globe, the thatched wooden theatre rebuilt on the bank. Tate Modern’s chimney is the next building along.

II
Chapter two · 4:45 pm – 6:40 pm

Tate Modern at sunset

A power station turned into an instrument, then the sun going down from the roof, both free.

4:45 pm

Turbine Hall: Tarek Atoui, Hold

Free · opened 13 Oct

This year’s giant Turbine Hall commission opened on Tuesday, so it is the newest big art piece in London this weekend. Atoui has turned the vast hall into a playable space, full of custom wind instruments, percussion and sculptural objects. Hidden microphones sample the instruments and the noise of the crowd, and software layers them back into the room. Live musicians perform in it during the run. Walk in at the top of the ramp and let it build around you.

Good to know
  • Tate Modern is open until 9:00 pm on Fridays and Saturdays, and the Turbine Hall and the collection are free.
  • If you want a ticketed show, Frida: The Making of an Icon is on, but it needs 90 minutes and booking. Skip it today.
  • Ask at the desk whether a live performance is scheduled for Saturday afternoon.

5:40 pm

Level 10 for sunset

Free · open till 9:00 pm Sat

Take the lift in the Blavatnik Building, the twisted brick pyramid, to Level 10. It is a free open-air deck that wraps all the way round. St Paul’s dome sits straight across the river, with the Shard, the City towers and the Thames bends spread out around it. Sunset is at 6:03 pm. Stay for the blue light afterwards, when the bridges light up.

Good to know
  • The lift goes up only from Levels 0 to 4 of the Blavatnik Building.
  • The Level 10 café does coffee and tea if it is chilly.
  • No booking is needed, though there can be a short lift queue around sunset.

6:15 pm

Millennium Bridge & St Paul’s

The photo of the day

Straight out of Tate Modern, onto the steel footbridge (the one the Death Eaters wreck in Half-Blood Prince). Walk to the middle as St Paul’s lights up in front of you. Go to the far end and back if you like, then follow the Thames path east, back the way you came, to Borough. It takes about 20 minutes, past the lit-up Globe.

Chapter three · 6:45 pm – 8:15 pm

Dinner at Oma

A Michelin star, a five-star Time Out review, and the train home five minutes away.

6:45 pm

Oma, Borough Market

Book 6:45 pm

David Carter’s Greek-ish restaurant, upstairs on Bedale Street, built around a live-fire grill. It has a Michelin star, and Time Out gave it five stars, calling it “wildly imaginative”. You order lots of sharing plates. Start with the breads and the labneh with salt-cod XO sauce, add a few mezze, then one thing from the grill between you. It’s lively and close-packed, with trains rumbling past the windows, so it is buzzy rather than hushed.

Good to know
  • Open 12:00 to 11:00 pm on Saturdays. Booking is essential, and it is done directly with the restaurant, not through Michelin.
  • Michelin and reviewers rate it £££, so expect a proper dinner bill rather than a market one.
  • Address: 2 to 4 Bedale Street, SE1 9AL, a five-minute walk from the Thameslink platforms.
  • No table? Downstairs is Agora, its sister taverna with the same grill. It is walk-in only and cheaper.
